# 10. FRONTEND & UI/UX COMPLIANCE FINDINGS

- **5 Psychological Laws:** Jakob, Hick, Proximity, Miller (7±2), Von Restorff fully implemented.
- **Micro-Standards:** 2:1 button padding, single-column forms, nested radius ($R_{inner} = R_{outer} - P$).
- **Color Invariant:** Strict absence of `#000000` and `#ffffff` in body; warm editorial stone/slate tones used.
- **Skeleton Shimmer:** Zero blank loading spinners; smooth skeleton loading states across all list views.
